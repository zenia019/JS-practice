Our Story

A cup made to slow you down

Beans & Bliss started as a single roaster and a handful of chairs. Today it's still that same idea, just bigger — a warm room, low light, and coffee poured slowly enough to matter.

We source our beans in small batches and roast often, because a fresher bean makes a better cup — and a better cup makes a better morning.
